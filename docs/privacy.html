Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026 · Contact: support@mylune.app

Lumi is a local-first period and cycle tracker for iOS. This policy describes what the app stores, what may leave your device, and your choices.

Overview

Lumi (“we”, “the app”) is a period and cycle tracker. This policy explains what the app stores, what leaves your device, and your choices. By using Lumi you agree to this policy.

What stays on your device

  • Period and cycle dates you log
  • Flow, symptoms, mood, energy, sleep, and notes
  • Predictions and insights computed on your phone
  • App settings (reminders, appearance, optional app lock)
  • Optional on-device AI chat and downloaded Gemma model files

We do not operate a Lumi cloud that receives your health logs. This data stays on your iPhone unless you export or delete it.

What we do not do

  • No ads or ad tracking
  • No third-party analytics SDKs
  • No sale of personal data
  • No cross-app tracking
  • No Lumi account or health server that receives your logs

Network use

Tracking works offline. The only optional network feature is downloading the Gemma AI model from Hugging Face when you choose Settings → AI → Download. That connection goes directly to Hugging Face; Lumi does not proxy it. Hugging Face may log standard connection metadata under their policy.

Security

Data is stored in the app sandbox using SwiftData. iOS Data Protection applies at rest. Optional Face ID / Touch ID lock uses Apple LocalAuthentication on device only.

Your choices

  • Export JSON or CSV in Settings
  • Delete all data on device in Settings
  • Remove the AI model without deleting cycle data
  • Turn off reminders at any time

Medical disclaimer

Lumi is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition. It helps you notice personal patterns only. Talk to a clinician for medical decisions.

Children

Lumi is intended for users old enough to track menstruation. Minors should use the app with a parent or guardian.

Changes & contact

We may update this policy when the app changes. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
