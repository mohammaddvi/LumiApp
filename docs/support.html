Support

We’re here to help with Lumi on iPhone.

How to get help

Email us at support@mylune.app with a short description of what you’re seeing. Include your iOS version and Lumi version (Settings → About) when you can.

We don’t have access to your cycle data — it’s local-first on your device — so screenshots of Settings (with personal details hidden) are often the most useful.

FAQ

How do I export or delete my data?

Open Settings. Use Export for JSON or CSV. Use Delete all data to wipe cycle logs on this iPhone. Deleting the app also removes local data unless you have a device backup.

Does Lumi cost anything?

No. Every feature is free, including Ask Lumi (on-device AI chat) and Insights. There are no ads and no subscriptions.

Why does Lumi download a Gemma model?

Ask Lumi runs on your iPhone. When you choose Settings → AI → Download, the Gemma model is fetched from Hugging Face (litert-community). Lumi does not proxy that download or send your health logs to a Lumi server. You can remove the model later without deleting cycle data.

How does Face ID / Touch ID lock work?

Optional app lock uses Apple’s LocalAuthentication on device only. Biometrics never leave your iPhone. Unlock fails safely if Face ID or Touch ID isn’t available — you can turn the lock off in Settings after authenticating.

Is Lumi medical advice?

No. Lumi helps you notice patterns in your own cycle. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition. Talk to a clinician for medical decisions.
