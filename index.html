Most trackers send intimate health data to their servers. Lumi doesn’t have any. Everything you log is stored on your device.

Read the privacy policy
  • No account or sign-up
  • No cloud, no health server
  • No ads, analytics or tracking
  • AI answers made on your phone
  • Optional Face ID app lock
  • Export, import or delete anytime

Lumi is a wellness app, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, and ovulation estimates are not a form of birth control. Talk to a clinician for medical decisions.
