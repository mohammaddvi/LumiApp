Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

In short: Lumi is local-first. Period logs, symptoms, notes and predictions stay on your iPhone. We do not run ads, sell data, use analytics, or operate a health cloud for this app.

Overview

Lumi (“we”, “the app”) is a period and cycle tracker for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what the app stores, what leaves your device, and your choices. By using Lumi you agree to this policy.

What Lumi stores on your device

  • Period and cycle dates you log
  • Flow intensity, symptoms, mood, energy, sleep and notes
  • Predictions and insights generated on your device
  • App preferences (cycle and period length, reminders, appearance, optional app lock) and the name Lumi calls you
  • Optional on-device AI chat history and downloaded Gemma model files

This data stays on your device unless you export or delete it. Like other app data, it is included in your own iPhone backups (iCloud Backup or your computer), which are controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

What we do not collect

  • No Lumi account and no server that receives your health logs
  • No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No sale or sharing of personal data

Because we never receive your data, we cannot view, recover or hand it over to anyone.

Network use

Lumi works offline for tracking. The only optional network feature is downloading the Gemma AI model from Hugging Face (litert-community) when you tap Download in the Ask tab. That request goes directly to Hugging Face; we do not proxy it, and no health data is sent. Hugging Face may log standard connection metadata under their own policy. The model is provided by Google under the Gemma Terms of Use.

Once downloaded, the AI runs entirely on your device. Your questions and its answers are not sent anywhere.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. They do not use a push server.

Security

Cycle data is stored with SwiftData in the app’s private sandbox, protected at rest by iOS Data Protection. The optional Face ID / Touch ID app lock uses Apple’s LocalAuthentication framework on device; Lumi never sees or stores biometric data.

Your choices

  • Export your data as JSON or CSV in Settings, and import a JSON export back
  • Delete all data on the device in Settings
  • Remove the downloaded AI model without deleting cycle data
  • Turn reminders off at any time
  • Delete the app to remove all of its data from the device

Medical disclaimer

Lumi helps you notice patterns in your own cycle. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Ovulation estimates are not a form of birth control. Talk to a clinician for medical decisions.

Age

Lumi is intended for users old enough to track menstruation. Parents or guardians should supervise use by minors.

Changes & contact

We may update this policy when the app changes, and will update the date above. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
